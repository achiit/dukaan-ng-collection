Why I opened this shop

For years my family had to go far to buy decent clothes at a fair price. My cousin and I kept saying Jawhar needed one shop where a husband and wife could both find what they like. In 2023 we finally opened N.G Collection on H.H. Road.

I stand at the counter most days. I know which shirt sells before a wedding and which kurti sells before a festival, so I stock accordingly and keep the prices fair.

Come in, touch the cloth, try it on. If you cannot visit, send us a WhatsApp message and we will send photos.
